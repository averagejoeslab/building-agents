\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

An LLM agent is a model wrapped in a harness, and a harness is five mechanistic primitives: \prim{Input}, \prim{Context}, \prim{Model interface}, \prim{Output} and \prim{Control flow}. What distinguishes one harness from another is the design decisions made within those primitives, and what makes a harness fit to run is the answers it gives to six questions asked of each. Six harnesses in wide use, deconstructed from their sources, fit this description with nothing left over. The vocabulary gives an engineer five places to look before blaming the model, and gives a benchmark a way to say which harness produced a score.
