\section{The Minimal Harnesses}
\label{app:minimal}

The five programs of \S\ref{sec:primitives}, complete, without their dependency headers. Each is the previous one plus one primitive. They are \texttt{harness/1\_model\_interface.py} to \texttt{harness/quark.py} in the companion repository \citep{course}, and the transcripts quoted in \S\ref{sec:primitives} are from the recorded runs there. The companion repository adds the production concerns of \S\ref{sec:levels} to the last program one question at a time, and later replaces \texttt{request\_response} with a function that runs a local model and translates between the Messages format and the model's chat template, leaving the other four primitives unchanged.
