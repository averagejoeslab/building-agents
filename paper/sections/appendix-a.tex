\section{The Minimal Harnesses}
\label{app:minimal}

The five programs of \S\ref{sec:primitives}, complete, without their dependency headers. Each is the previous one plus one primitive, and the transcripts quoted in \S\ref{sec:primitives} are from recorded runs of these programs. They are published with the paper. Replacing \texttt{request\_response} in the last program with a function that runs a local model and translates between the Messages format and the model's chat template leaves the other four primitives unchanged, which is the porting property of \S\ref{sec:vocabulary}.
