\section{Conclusion}
An LLM agent is a model wrapped in a harness, and a harness is five mechanistic primitives: \prim{Input}, \prim{Context}, \prim{Model interface}, \prim{Output} and \prim{Control flow}. Harnesses differ in the design decisions made within those primitives, and are made fit to run by the answers they give to six questions asked of each. Six harnesses in wide use fit this description with nothing left over. The vocabulary gives an engineer five places to look before blaming the model, and a benchmark a way to say which harness produced a score.
