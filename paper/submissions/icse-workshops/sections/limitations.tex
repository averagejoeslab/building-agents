\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
\paragraph{What would refute the claim} The position fails if a component of a working harness is found that (i) satisfies none of the five definitions, (ii) is not the answer to one of the six concerns within a primitive, and (iii) is not a nested harness. We invite reports of such a component. Learned components inside the harness and model-generated control flow arose in the six and were placed by the rules; search and decoding loops did not arise and remain untested.

\paragraph{Selection and sources} Six harnesses, five of them coding agents; harnesses for browsing, voice or robotics were not examined. Claude Code's deconstruction rests on documentation, which states what the harness does but not always how. Every assignment is pinned to a commit or a date, and several of the six change weekly.

\paragraph{Who made the assignments} No second, independent decomposition was made, so the agreement between two people applying the definitions is unmeasured.

\paragraph{No experimental test} The paper does not vary one primitive at a time and measure the effect; \cite{fan2026empirical} do this for three decisions, and a study across all five primitives is future work.
