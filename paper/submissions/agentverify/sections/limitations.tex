\section{Limitations and Further Work}
\label{sec:limitations}
\paragraph{Further work} The deconstruction shows the five cover what exists. It does not show which implementation of a primitive is better, for which model, on which task. With the harness stated in five parts that comparison is well-posed: hold the model and four primitives fixed, vary one, measure. It would both use the framework and test it, since a result that cannot be attributed to the primitive varied is a failure of the framework that reading source cannot show.

\paragraph{Selection and sources} Six harnesses, five of them coding agents; harnesses for browsing, voice or robotics were not examined. Claude Code's deconstruction rests on documentation, which states what the harness does but not always how. Every assignment is pinned to a commit or a date.

\paragraph{Who made the assignments} No second, independent decomposition was made, so the agreement between two people applying the definitions is unmeasured.

\paragraph{No experimental test} The paper does not vary one primitive at a time and measure the effect; \cite{fan2026empirical} do this for three decisions, and a study across all five primitives is future work.
